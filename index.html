What the sourcing marks mean
Official Announced by Warner Bros, HBO, Bloomsbury or the Wizarding World channels themselves.
Confirmed Not an official announcement, but reported and stood up by the trade press such as Variety, Deadline or The Hollywood Reporter.
Rumor Circulating without a named, reliable source. Treat it as unproven.
Leak Material that escaped before release, such as set images, scripts or unlisted footage.
Paparazzi Photos taken off set or in public by press photographers rather than published by the studio.

Prophet Watch gathers Harry Potter news twice a day, in the morning and at the end of the afternoon, Rome time. Every entry keeps the link to where it came from, so nothing has to be taken on trust. The sourcing mark reflects how well stood up a story was when it was filed, not how likely it is to be true.
